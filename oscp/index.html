OSCP Exploitation Reference

Complete operational task index — every procedure and task, searchable by service or technique.

A task-oriented study knowledge base and exam playbook. Start with the Attack Strategy Map to compare methods by prerequisites and open a self-contained study entry. Use the stage procedures and cheatsheets for operational lookup. Practical mastery remains tracked separately from publication.

Learning resources

  • AD Notes — focused investigations: a short quick reference, where to look, annotated output, expected behavior and evidence-driven next checks.
  • Interactive strategy workspace: expandable workflows, recognition checks, commands and evidence-driven next decisions; attack-path visualizer for explicitly recorded investigation steps.
  • Attack Strategy Map: the complete documented method inventory, prerequisite-based selection and links between capability transitions.
  • Discovery-to-recognition workflows: where to investigate before choosing an attack.
  • Recognition practice: unanswered paper cases for testing clue discovery and prerequisite reasoning.
  • Self-contained method guides: explanations, prerequisite checks, commands/variants, worked examples, verification and failure-driven alternatives.
  • Master Notes: attack reasoning, necessary mechanics, recognition clues and failure interpretation.
  • Speedrun breakdown: attack tasks, practical outcomes and the tooling map for accelerated study.
  • getSH coverage map: source snapshot, route/topic accounting, corrections and remaining speedrun gaps.
  • Cheatsheet index: compact, executable commands and decision cues for immediate lookup.
  • Use the stage and quick-task indexes below for complete operational procedures.

Operational flow: discovery and scanning → service enumeration → initial access → shells/transfers/credentials → local privilege escalation → AD/lateral movement → pivoting/tunneling → evidence/reporting.

Study flow: use the grouped study sequence. Numbered blocks remain stable lookup units; related blocks are learned as complete attack paths.

How to use the reference

  1. For study, choose the objective in the strategy map and compare its method prerequisites.
  2. Read the selected method's checks, explanation, example, variants and next transitions.
  3. For scoped practice, open its linked operational procedure and establish the required access/configuration.
  4. Replace every placeholder and use the baseline or variant matching the observed context.
  5. Interpret decisive output and failure cues before claiming the intended effect.
  6. After a state change, follow the linked next methods and re-enumerate the new capability.

Target IPs, credentials, flags, transcripts, licensed course text and machine solutions stay in private lab records.
